A temporary exhibition

The River
Workers

Ferrymen, rope makers and lamp lighters: the trades that kept the Harwick running, and the people who did them.

A city that forgot its own river.

For two centuries most people in Harwick earned their living within a mile of the water. Our stores hold more than four hundred objects from that working river, and almost none of them have been on display since 1983.

This exhibition brings them out, alongside oral histories recorded this year with the last ferry crew and the families of the rope walk. It asks visitors one question at the door: who built the river city you walked here through?
