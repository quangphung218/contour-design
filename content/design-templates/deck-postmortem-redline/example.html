CAUSE

What we assumed, and what was true.

“Halving the pool size is safe: we never use more than 40 connections.”

We never used more than 40 on average. At peak, card auth briefly holds 70+ while it waits on the network, so requests queued until they timed out.

the dashboard showed 1-min averages, not peaks
