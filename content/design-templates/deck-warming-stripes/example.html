Ellesby Town Council · Climate briefing · November 2026

Ellesby is 1.4 °C warmer than in 1950.

What that means for heat, floods and the people most exposed, and five actions we can afford.

How to read the stripes

One stripe per year, 1950 to 2025. Blue cooler, red warmer.

Each colour is that year's average temperature against the 1961–1990 average for our weather station. Eleven of the last twelve stripes are red. (Illustrative series for this sample deck.)

Who is most exposed

residents over 75 living alone, most in the old town's top-floor flats

380

6
