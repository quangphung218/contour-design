Questions

When does my box arrive?

Boxes ship on the 5th of each month. Join before the 1st to get the current month's harvests.

Can I change teas I don't like?

Tell us what you didn't enjoy and we'll swap it for a reserve tea from the same month.

Are the tins recyclable?

Yes, steel tins and paper labels. Or send five back with your next return label and we refill them.

Do growers get a fair price?

We pay a fixed price per kilo agreed before the harvest, published on every tasting card.
