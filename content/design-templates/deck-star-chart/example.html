Moon pairings are the easiest way in: find the Moon, and the bright point beside it is the planet.

What you need

Ten minutes of dark eyes beats any gadget.

Kit

  • Any 8×42 or 10×50 binoculars
  • A red torch, or red cellophane on a phone
  • A folding chair, facing away from lights

Habits

  • No bright screens for ten minutes
  • Look slightly beside faint objects
  • Keep a one-line log: date, time, what you saw
