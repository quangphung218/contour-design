Why

Three causes we could fix ourselves.

1

Everyone waited for the same queue.

A repeat prescription and chest pain waited in one line, in arrival order.

2

Triage came after the longest wait.

Nurses saw patients 38 minutes in, too late to redirect them.

3

Patients went to any free doctor.

So 1 in 4 were re-seen by their own GP later in the week.
