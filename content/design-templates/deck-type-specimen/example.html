Pairing · Corran Bold headline, Corran Regular text

Long reads deserve a quiet voice.

Corran was drawn for the twentieth paragraph, not the first. Its x-height is generous without shouting, its serifs short enough to stay crisp at 14 pixels, and its italic is a true cursive for emphasis that reads as a change of voice. Figures come in lining and old-style sets.

  • x-height0.52 em: larger than most book faces, for screens
  • spacingslightly loose at text sizes, tightened in the Display cut
  • figurestabular lining by default, old-style via a feature
  • languages214, including Vietnamese and Welsh
