# Code of conduct

Contour follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work.

## Expected

- Respectful, patient replies, including to newcomers and to questions
  asked in imperfect English.
- Feedback on code and ideas, not on people.
- Accepting a maintainer's decision on scope, and saying so if you disagree.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, sexual language or imagery.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull
request. Reports are handled privately by the maintainer. The maintainer
may edit or remove content and block accounts that break these rules.

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions.
